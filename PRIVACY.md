# Wide Margin replies – Privacy Policy

Wide Margin replies is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Wide Margin (@WideMarginmath) to read public comments posted on the channel's own videos and to reply to them.

- Data accessed: public comments on Wide Margin videos (author display name, comment text, date) through the YouTube Data API.
- Use: only to write and post replies on behalf of the channel.
- Storage: only the IDs and texts of replies sent are kept in a private log. No personal data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
- No other Google account data is accessed.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Wide Margin replies' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: via the Wide Margin YouTube channel.
